Harova Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Harova ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Harova mobile application or related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your mobile number and information needed to verify your identity using a one-time password (OTP).
  • Customer information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address, including city and postal PIN code.
  • Order information: Products selected, quantities, cart contents, order totals, order history, and order or payment status.
  • Payment-related information: Information needed to initiate and confirm payments. Payment providers may process payment details according to their own policies.
  • Technical information: Information that may be generated when you use the app, such as diagnostic information, device or app details, and security logs, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information, as necessary, to:

  • Create and verify your account.
  • Display products and manage your shopping cart.
  • Process orders and arrange delivery.
  • Initiate payments and confirm payment status.
  • Communicate about orders, account access, and customer support.
  • Maintain app security, prevent misuse, and resolve technical issues.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. OTP Verification

Harova uses OTP-based verification for account access. Your mobile number and verification-related information may be processed by our OTP service provider, MSG91, to deliver verification messages and help authenticate your account.

4. Payments

Harova uses Razorpay for payment processing. When you make a payment, Razorpay may collect and process payment information in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms. Payment confirmation or transaction-related information may be shared with Harova to process and manage your order.

Please review Razorpay's privacy information at Razorpay Privacy Policy.

5. When We Share Information

We may share relevant information with service providers when needed to operate the app and fulfil your requests. These may include:

  • OTP and authentication providers, such as MSG91.
  • Payment processors, such as Razorpay.
  • Hosting, backend, technical support, and infrastructure providers used to operate the service.
  • Delivery or logistics providers, where necessary to deliver your orders.
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by applicable law or to protect legal rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for money. Information is shared only as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as otherwise permitted or required by law.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored and processed by Harova and the service providers used to operate the app. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain order and transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect our rights. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the purpose for which it is used.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask questions about how it is handled. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Harova is a shopping service and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services to provide features such as OTP verification and payment processing. Those providers may handle information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the applicable policies of services you use.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
