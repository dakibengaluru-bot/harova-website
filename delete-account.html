Harova Account Deletion Request

Harova respects your privacy and provides a way for customers to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to support@pinebow.in with the subject Harova Account Deletion Request.

Please include the mobile number associated with your Harova account, including the country code, so we can identify your account. Do not send your OTP or password.

What happens next?

We will verify your request and contact you if additional information is needed. After verification, we will process the account deletion request in accordance with applicable law and our data-retention obligations.

Data deletion and retention

Personal information associated with your account will be deleted where applicable. Certain order, transaction, tax, accounting, or other records may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes for the period required by law.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact support@pinebow.in.
